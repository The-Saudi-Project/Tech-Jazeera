# Brag Plan: Valizent CRM

## What is this app?
An internal ERP for a manpower supply & trading company in Saudi Arabia —
employees, clients, deployments/mobilisations, attendance, documents,
quotations, payroll, leave, financial requests, approvals, and a real profit
dashboard — built to replace Excel sheets, WhatsApp coordination, and paper
tracking as the company's actual daily operating system.

## The angle
The joke-free, earnest premium-product angle: the "before" is the universal
small-business stack (Excel + WhatsApp + paper), stated completely flatly.
The "after" is one coherent system — real workflow (a client requirement
becomes a tracked, approved deployment), real bilingual support (true Arabic
RTL, not a translated label), and a calm, premium dark-mode UI. No jokes, no
overstatement — the product is the whole point, and it earns the "serious
software" treatment because it actually is serious software running a real
operation.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Three flat, deadpan lines, one at a time, stark white-on-black, no music yet:
"Excel sheets." / "WhatsApp groups." / "Paper files." — the unglamorous
reality this app replaced (grounded in the project's own README/CLAUDE.md:
"Replaces Excel sheets, WhatsApp coordination, and paper tracking").

## Key moments (the middle)
- The brand reveal: "Valizent CRM" wordmark settles in over a recreated dark
  dashboard — module chips tick across (Employees · Clients · Deployments ·
  Payroll · Approvals), grounded in the real module list.
- The workflow: a Requirements-board kanban card ("Client requirement") slides
  from "In Review" into "Approved" and locks in with a satisfying thud —
  this app's real Coordinator-Workflow pipeline (a client requirement becomes
  a tracked, approved Mobilisation), not an invented feature.
- The bilingual flip: the same detail panel mirrors live from English to
  real Arabic RTL — grounded in the project's documented "real RTL for
  Arabic... verified with a screenshot" work.

## Outro / punchline
"Valizent CRM." / "Built for the operation, not the pitch deck." — product
name, then a quiet, confident tagline. Hold on the wordmark over the soft
indigo glow background that's a real token in the app's own CSS
(`--bg-wash`). No punchline joke — restraint is the punchline.

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: a client requirement lands on the Requirements board (Kanban
   "In Review" column).
2. Key action: it's approved and moves to "Approved" — the real
   Coordinator-Workflow handoff this app documents (requirement →
   Mobilisation → Deployment).
3. Result: the same record, now live, shown bilingually (EN → AR, real RTL)
   — the detail that proves this isn't a mockup of a generic CRM, it's this
   one.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film for an earnest, real
  operational tool — confidence through restraint, not hype.
- Interpretation: slow-ish holds (4-6s per scene), light-to-medium type
  weight, soft crossfades, minimal SFX (2-3 total), a steady/clean music bed
  kept low. No ALL CAPS, no hard cuts, no jokes landing at the product's
  expense.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: rgb(4, 7, 22) — the app's own `.dark` theme `--color-bg`
  (near slate-950, indigo-tinted)
- Surface/cards: rgb(17, 22, 44) — `.dark` `--color-surface`
- Accent/primary: rgb(129, 128, 246) — `.dark` `--color-primary` (brighter
  indigo, tuned to read on dark)
- Text: rgb(236, 238, 248) — `.dark` `--color-text`
- Muted text: rgb(148, 158, 190) — `.dark` `--color-muted`
- Success accent: rgb(45, 205, 110) — `.dark` `--color-success`
- Background wash: soft indigo/violet/cyan/pink radial glows from the app's
  own `--bg-wash` dark-mode value — use restrained, not full saturation
- Display font: "Plus Jakarta Sans" (the app's `font-heading`)
- Body font: Inter (the app's `font-sans`)
- Strongest visual element: the app's own dark-mode token system (soft
  indigo-tinted near-black, never pure black) + the Kanban requirements
  board's card-to-column motion

Note: all on-screen UI content (client names, card labels, stat numbers) are
plausible fictional stand-ins — no real client, employee, or production data
appears anywhere in this video.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, restrained corporate bed; sparse, tasteful SFX only at real
  motion moments
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` ("steady and
  clean" — the bundled track explicitly recommended for `polished`/`cinematic`)
- Music treatment: silent under the hook (first ~1.0s), fades in low
  (~0.12) as the hook's third line settles, rises to 0.30 for the brand
  reveal, holds through the middle, fades to 0 over the final ~1.5s under
  the outro hold
- Music cue guidance: preset read from
  `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md`
  (tempo ≈ 109.96 BPM). Two strong-cue locks planned:
  - Kanban card lands in "Approved" → strong cue **9.29s** (0.97, strong_beat)
  - Bilingual EN→AR flip lands → strong cue **17.47s** (0.99, strong_beat)
  No other moment is forced onto a cue — the hook's three lines deliberately
  ignore the beat grid (tempo is ~110 BPM / a beat every ~0.54s, far faster
  than the reading-time floor for short lines; forcing it would rush them).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the background wash glow/opacity
  breathe gently with music RMS during the brand-reveal and middle scenes.
  No waveform/equalizer visuals, no strobing.
- SFX posture: sparse — exactly 3 cues across the whole video, all soft
- Audio-coupled moments: the wordmark reveal (one soft announcement hit),
  the kanban card landing (card-place sound, beat-locked), the EN→AR flip
  (a soft switch sound, beat-locked)
- Restraint rule: no SFX under the hook or the outro hold — silence and a
  slow fade are the premium cue, not a sting

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 4.0s
Near-black full-bleed background (no cards, no chrome yet). Three short lines
appear centered, one replacing the last via a slow crossfade, sentence case,
light-to-medium weight, generous letter-spacing: "Excel sheets." → "WhatsApp
groups." → "Paper files." Each line settles for ~1.1s (comfortably over the
~0.8s short-label floor) before crossfading to the next.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three lines, one at a time, ~1.1s settle each,
crossfade (not cut) between them.
Audio intent: total quiet — let the absence of sound sell "the old way."
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: silent for the first ~1.0s, then fades in very low (~0.12) under the
third line only.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2 (slow crossfade into the dark dashboard, not
a hard cut — restraint carries through the transition too)

### Scene 2 — Reveal — 5.3s
Hard-earned dark dashboard surface fades up behind the "Valizent CRM"
wordmark, which settles top-center in the display font. Beneath it, five
module chips ("Employees", "Clients", "Deployments", "Payroll", "Approvals")
tick in left-to-right, each a small pill on the dark surface color, arriving
~0.25s apart. Behind them, a soft, mostly-static recreated dashboard card grid
(stat labels like "Active Deployments", "Workforce", "This Month" with
illustrative — not asserted — numbers) sits dimmed, out of focus, as texture
rather than the subject.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 5 module chips arrive left-to-right, ~0.25s
apart, each with a very quiet, soft arrival (no individual SFX — the chips
arrive too fast for 5 individual cues at this tone; one single soft
announcement sound covers the whole reveal instead).
Audio intent: quiet confidence — one clean emotional beat, not a flourish.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft announcement hit (e.g. `impact/impactBell_heavy_000`
or `interface/bong_001`, chosen at composition time) under the wordmark's
own settle point, ~0.2s into the scene.
Music: rises to 0.30, steady.
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Key moment: the workflow — 5.5s
Cut (soft crossfade) to a recreated Requirements board: three Kanban columns
("New", "In Review", "Approved") on dark cards. A single card — "Client
requirement — 3 Electricians" with a small fictional company name — sits in
"In Review" at scene start, then slides right into "Approved" and settles
with a soft downward "place" motion. A small green check badge fades onto
the card the instant it lands. Caption beneath, arriving after the card
settles: "A requirement becomes a tracked deployment."
Sequential/interaction: yes — explicit simulated drag/slide of one card from
column 2 to column 3, landing with a settle.
Audio intent: the one real "action lands" moment of the video — a quiet, solid
confirmation, not a celebration.
Audio-coupled idea: a card-place/card-slide sound (e.g. `casino/card-slide-3`
into `casino/card-place-2`, chosen at composition time) exactly at the card's
landing frame. // beat-locked: 9.29s
Music: steady at 0.30, no swell.
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Key moment: bilingual, for real — 4.5s
The same card/detail panel from Scene 3, now shown as a clean detail view
(label/value rows: client, requirement, status). At the ~2.7s mark, it
mirrors live: the whole panel flips from left-to-right English into
right-to-left Arabic — text re-flows and right-aligns, labels swap to Arabic
placeholders, the layout direction genuinely mirrors (not just translated
text sitting in an LTR frame). A small "EN" pill flips to "AR".
Sequential/interaction: yes — one simulated toggle action (EN→AR) producing a
true RTL mirror of the whole panel, not a cut to a second static frame.
Audio intent: a small, precise click — the detail that proves this is real
engineering, not a translated screenshot.
Audio-coupled idea: a soft switch/toggle sound (e.g. `interface/switch_004`,
chosen at composition time) at the exact flip frame. // beat-locked: 17.47s
Music: steady at 0.30, begins a slow fade in the last ~0.5s of this scene.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 2.7s
Cut to the near-black background wash alone (the soft indigo/violet glow,
no cards). "Valizent CRM" settles center, slightly larger than its Scene 2
appearance. Beneath it, one small line fades in and holds: "Built for the
operation, not the pitch deck." Hold to the end.
Sequential/interaction: none — one settle, then stillness.
Audio intent: let it go quiet. The silence is the confidence.
Audio-coupled idea: none — no SFX in this scene.
Music: fades from 0.30 to 0 across the full 2.7s.
Transition mood: — (final scene)

**Music mood for this video:** polished / steady and clean, never above 0.30
**Audio summary:** Silence under the hook, a single soft confirmation hit on
the brand reveal, a beat-locked card-landing sound on the workflow moment, a
beat-locked switch sound on the bilingual flip, then a full fade to silence
under the outro hold — three SFX cues total, music never above 0.30.
